# Brand and Trademark Policy

The purpose is to separate permission to learn or make an authorized noncommercial fork from permission to present a fork as the official Akronikl IT Nexus.

Reserved identity includes `AKRONIKL IT NEXUS`, `Akronikl Nexus`, the Akronikl mentor/character identity in project context, official logos/icons and designated brand assets.

A future code/content license does not automatically grant a right to imply official affiliation or endorsement.

Trademark rights are territorial. For a Russian-first project, the practical first path is similarity clearance and, if suitable, a Rospatent application in relevant classes. International expansion can later use national offices and/or WIPO's Madrid System based on an eligible basic mark.

Until registration, use consistent branding, preserve evidence of use, do not use `®`, and conduct a professional clearance search before major branding spend.
