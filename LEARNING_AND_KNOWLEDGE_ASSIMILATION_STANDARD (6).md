# ADR-0008 — Free Education and Protection from Commercial Repackaging

**Status:** accepted  
**Date:** 2026-09-17

Core learning remains free. The platform must not depend on a mandatory paid AI subscription. A future public license should support learning and approved noncommercial uses while restricting commercial copying, repackaging and resale. If commercial use is restricted, the project will not describe the license as OSI Open Source.
