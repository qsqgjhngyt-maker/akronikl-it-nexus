# Open Education Mission

## Why Akronikl IT Nexus exists

Akronikl IT Nexus is not intended to be a storefront of paid courses. It is designed as a **system for acquiring engineering knowledge**, available regardless of a learner's ability to pay.

The primary audience is Russian-speaking, while the platform is designed to be international and multilingual: **Russian-first, not Russian-only**.

## Non-negotiable principles

1. Core learning content — theory, full theory, exercises, labs, projects, Skill Graph and basic learning features — must not require a tuition paywall.
2. Free access for learners does not grant third parties a right to commercially repackage and resell the project.
3. Akronikl exists to reduce the barrier to understanding: reframe explanations, diagnose errors, connect a topic to prior knowledge and bring the learner back to precise technical language.
4. Course quality is measured by understanding and transfer, not page count.
5. The platform must remain useful without paid cloud AI. Cloud functions may have infrastructure limits, but the core learning experience must not collapse without them.
6. Multilingual support is an architectural property, not a late add-on.

## Learning formula

`prerequisites → understandable explanation → deep theory → visual/system model → experiment → practice → comprehension check → skill connections → next step`.

## Mission statement

> **High-quality engineering education should not begin with the question: “Can you afford it?”**

> **Nexus exists for education, not for reselling education.**
