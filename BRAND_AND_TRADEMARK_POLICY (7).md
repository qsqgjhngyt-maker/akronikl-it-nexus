# ADR-0009 — Multi-Layer Rights and Licensing Model

**Status:** target model accepted; license activation deferred until rights audit  
**Date:** 2026-09-17

Do not apply one blanket license to the entire repository. Target mapping: software → noncommercial source-available software license (PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0 candidate); original educational content/docs → CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 candidate; brand/mascot/logo → reserved under separate brand policy; third-party components → their own licenses; secrets → never published. No root `LICENSE` is added until ownership and provenance are verified.
