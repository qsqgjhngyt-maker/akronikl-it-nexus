# Target Licensing Model

## Foundation decision

Core learning should be free, while automatic commercial repackaging should not be permitted. MIT/Apache/BSD are therefore not suitable as the primary project license because they allow commercial use. Since OSI Open Source requires freedom of commercial use, the intended model should be described as **source-available / free educational**, not OSI Open Source.

## Software candidate

**Preferred candidate: PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0.** It is software-oriented and permits use, modification and distribution for permitted noncommercial purposes. Before activation we must review its broad educational-institution permission against our exact “no paid copycat service” goal.

PolyForm Strict is not preferred because it prevents redistribution and derivative works, which would block healthy noncommercial community collaboration.

## Learning content and documentation candidate

**Preferred candidate: CC BY-NC-SA 4.0.** It permits noncommercial sharing and adaptation with attribution and ShareAlike. Creative Commons itself recommends not using CC licenses for software, so software and content stay separate.

CC grants are intended to be irrevocable while the license terms are followed, so rights provenance must be verified before applying the license.

## Brand and third-party materials

Official names, logos, mascot/mentor identity and designated brand assets are excluded from the general content license. Third-party components remain under their own licenses.

## Commercial exceptions

The rights holder may grant separate written permission for a specific commercial use later. That does not create a duty to commercialize the platform or place core education behind a paywall.
