# ADR-0005-CONTENT_DEPTH_BEFORE_SCALE — Establish quality before scaling content

**Status:** Accepted

## Decision
Create and approve three reference-quality C++ lessons before mass-producing or migrating the remaining content.

## Rationale
This reduces the risk of scaling shallow, repetitive theory across dozens of disciplines.

## Consequences
This decision is part of the Foundation architecture contract. Changes require a new ADR rather than a silent rewrite of history.
