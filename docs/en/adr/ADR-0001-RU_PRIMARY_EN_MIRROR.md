# ADR-0001-RU_PRIMARY_EN_MIRROR — Russian as normative documentation, English as a secondary mirror

**Status:** Accepted

## Decision
Russian is the primary source of truth for documentation and terminology. The English version is maintained as a required secondary mirror.

## Rationale
This preserves precision in the primary development language while keeping the platform internationally accessible.

## Consequences
This decision is part of the Foundation architecture contract. Changes require a new ADR rather than a silent rewrite of history.
