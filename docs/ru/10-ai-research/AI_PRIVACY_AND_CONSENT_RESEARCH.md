# AI Research Privacy & Consent

Operational data and research data are separate purposes.

Stages:
1. synthetic fixtures;
2. developer-owned internal pilot;
3. consenting adult pilot;
4. formal diploma study under university requirements.

If minors participate, a separate consent/ethics protocol is required.

Research export:
- pseudonymous participant id;
- no login/contact identity;
- no auth secrets.

Before external AI processing:
- review retention/training settings;
- minimize payload;
- document provider/processing;
- disclose relevant use to participants.
