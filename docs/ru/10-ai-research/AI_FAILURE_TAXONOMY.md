# AI Failure Taxonomy

- F-CORRECT — incorrect reasoning/fact.
- F-GROUND — unsupported by evidence.
- F-PERSONAL — ignores learner state.
- F-PREREQ — assumes missing prerequisite.
- F-CONTEXT — wrong/missing context.
- F-RAG — retrieval failure.
- F-HALLUCINATION — invented API/lesson/file/fact.
- F-TEACH — pedagogically inappropriate.
- F-SAFETY — privacy/secret/tool violation.
- F-OPER — timeout/provider failure.
- F-MODELSTATE — misleading mastery estimate.

One case may have multiple tags.
