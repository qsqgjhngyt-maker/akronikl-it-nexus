# Research Ethics Checklist

Before participant study:
- research question/protocol frozen;
- university requirements checked;
- participant information prepared;
- informed consent prepared;
- age eligibility defined;
- withdrawal process defined;
- pseudonymization tested;
- auth secrets excluded;
- external AI processing disclosed;
- retention/deletion defined;
- raw dataset access limited;
- publication reviewed for re-identification risk.

This checklist is governance, not a replacement for university/legal requirements.
