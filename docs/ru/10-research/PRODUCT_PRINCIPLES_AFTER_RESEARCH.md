# Product Principles After Competitive Research

1. **Learn by building, but do not outsource learning to generation.**
2. **Projects are evidence, not decoration.**
3. **Progress means competence evidence, not only content completion.**
4. **AI explains and guides before it replaces user effort.**
5. **Cross-device continuity is a product invariant.**
6. **Engineering history matters: checkpoints, revisions, audit, tests.**
7. **Recommendations must be explainable and overridable.**
8. **AI personalization must be measurable.**
9. **Privacy-minimized context beats indiscriminate data collection.**
10. **Borrow patterns, never copy product identity.**
11. **Do not claim uniqueness where only public evidence is incomplete.**
12. **Research results can change the roadmap.**
