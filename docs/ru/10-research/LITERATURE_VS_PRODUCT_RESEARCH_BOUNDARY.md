# Literature vs Product Research Boundary

## Product research answers
- what current products publicly offer;
- how they position workflows;
- what market patterns are visible.

Sources: official product/docs/help pages.

## Academic literature answers
- what algorithms/methods are known;
- what empirical evidence exists;
- learning science;
- knowledge tracing;
- tutoring effectiveness;
- RAG/LLM evaluation;
- ethics/statistics.

Sources: peer-reviewed papers, books, standards and reputable proceedings.

## Thesis rule
A competitor marketing page must not be used as scientific evidence that a method is effective.

Likewise, an academic paper does not prove that a particular competitor implements a feature.

These evidence types remain separate.
