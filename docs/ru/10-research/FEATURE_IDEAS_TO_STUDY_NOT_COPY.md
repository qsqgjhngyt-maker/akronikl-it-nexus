# Feature Ideas to Study — Not Copy

## Roadmap UX
Observed broadly across Coddy/Codecademy/DataCamp.

Nexus adaptation:
Skill Graph with prerequisite explanation and evidence state.

## Project-first learning
Strongly represented by Hyperskill and AI builders.

Nexus adaptation:
Project Studio project becomes longitudinal learning evidence.

## AI code feedback
Present in Codecademy/Sololearn/Coddy.

Nexus adaptation:
feedback tied to repeated-error taxonomy + Learner Model.

## Build-and-learn
Codecademy AI Builder and Coddy Build combine construction and explanation.

Nexus adaptation:
AI may explain user-authored project changes and map them to skills, while avoiding hidden wholesale generation in tutoring mode.

## Collaboration
Replit shows a strong cloud collaboration direction.

Nexus adaptation:
role/path ACL + educational team workflows + audit + review checkpoints.

## Mobile
Coddy/Sololearn/DataCamp validate mobile continuity.

Nexus adaptation:
view/progress/help everywhere; editing capabilities adapt to device.

## Rule
We adopt the underlying user need, not another product's UI, wording, visual hierarchy, mascot or implementation.
