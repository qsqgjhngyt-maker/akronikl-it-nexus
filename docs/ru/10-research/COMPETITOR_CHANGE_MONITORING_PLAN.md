# Competitor Change Monitoring Plan

## Why
Coddy, Replit, Codecademy и другие AI-продукты меняются быстрее обычного образовательного ПО.

## Watch areas
- AI tutor context/personalization;
- AI builders;
- skill maps/mastery;
- cloud project persistence;
- mobile workflows;
- collaboration/versioning;
- certificates/assessment;
- pricing/access;
- admin/CMS;
- privacy/data controls.

## Cadence
- lightweight scan: quarterly;
- full evidence refresh: twice per year;
- mandatory full refresh: before diploma finalization.

## Record
Every scan creates:
- date;
- source links;
- detected changes;
- impact on Nexus roadmap;
- whether any differentiation hypothesis was weakened/strengthened.

## Rule
Competitor evolution is not a reason to copy features automatically. It is an input to product/research decisions.
