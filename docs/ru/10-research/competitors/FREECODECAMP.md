# Competitor Profile — freeCodeCamp

## Publicly confirmed

freeCodeCamp is an open-source learning ecosystem with:
- structured curriculum;
- certification projects;
- certification definitions in repository metadata;
- large free-access learning model;
- community contribution and long-lived public curriculum.

Sources:
- `S-FCC-01`
- `S-FCC-02`

The current curriculum evolution publicly emphasizes:
- smaller certification milestones within a larger Full Stack path;
- projects and exams as evidence of completion;
- preservation/evolution of curriculum over time.

## Product lesson for Nexus

freeCodeCamp demonstrates the value of:
- transparent/open curriculum structure;
- durable learning records;
- projects as certification evidence;
- community-maintained educational content.

## Nexus differentiation hypothesis

Nexus can combine curriculum transparency with:
- persistent Project Studio state;
- cross-device project revision history;
- learner/skill model;
- integrated AI research layer;
- engineering lifecycle evidence.

Nexus should consider publishing selected curriculum schemas/standards openly even if the platform backend remains controlled.
