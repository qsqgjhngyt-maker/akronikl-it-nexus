# Competitor Profile — Scrimba

## Publicly confirmed

Scrimba's AI Engineer Path publicly includes interactive lessons, coding challenges and projects that learners can modify in the browser.

The path includes modules on:
- AI engineering;
- deployment;
- open-source models;
- embeddings/vector databases;
- AI agents;
- context engineering;
- Vercel AI SDK;
- MCP;
- multimodality.

Source: `S-SCRIMBA-01`.

## Product lesson for Nexus

Scrimba shows that modern AI-engineering content can be taught interactively and kept current with written technical companion docs.

Nexus should therefore:
- separate durable conceptual content from fast-changing provider/setup notes;
- keep model/provider-specific operational notes versioned;
- preserve a stable learning architecture even when AI tooling changes.

## Nexus differentiation hypothesis

Nexus aims to make the learner's own engineering project and longitudinal skill state part of the tutoring context, not only the lesson sandbox.
