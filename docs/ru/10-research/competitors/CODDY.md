# Competitor Profile — Coddy

## Publicly confirmed

Coddy positions itself as a free-to-start interactive coding platform with:
- 20+ programming languages;
- browser-based coding;
- roadmaps by job/goal;
- certificates;
- gamification/streaks/leaderboards;
- mobile apps;
- AI assistance inside learning;
- public user count above 5.2 million on the reviewed homepage.

Source: `S-CODDY-01`.

Coddy Build extends the product into an AI app-builder:
- prompt-driven app creation;
- live preview;
- code visibility;
- publish to live URL;
- backend/database/auth setup when needed;
- explanations/change summaries;
- mobile browser support with fuller toolset on desktop.

Source: `S-CODDY-02`.

## Product lesson for Nexus

Coddy shows that:
- browser execution + AI hints + gamification are no longer unusual differentiators;
- roadmap UX matters;
- learning and AI-assisted creation are converging;
- mobile continuity is expected.

## What Nexus should not imitate

- visual style;
- mascot mechanics;
- exact roadmap/course organization;
- wording/branding.

## Nexus differentiation hypothesis relative to Coddy

Nexus can focus deeper on:
- persistent engineering projects;
- checkpoints/revisions/audit;
- explicit multi-device project sync semantics;
- team ACL and engineering lifecycle;
- learner-model/skill-graph research;
- evidence-based AI personalization.

The reviewed public sources were not sufficient to conclude that Coddy lacks internal revisioning, ACL or similar infrastructure. The distinction above concerns publicly presented learner workflows.
