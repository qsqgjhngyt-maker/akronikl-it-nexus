# Contribution Rights Policy

External pull requests can introduce multiple copyright holders, which affects relicensing and enforcement.

Until the contributor model is approved, external contributions require manual origin/rights review and a representation that the contributor has the right to submit the material.

DCO and CLA should not be selected automatically. A DCO is useful for provenance but may not solve future relicensing; a CLA can grant broader rights but must be transparent and fair to contributors.

Future minimum: contributor attribution, provenance confirmation, inbound-license identification, prohibition on unauthorized proprietary material, and explicit consent if broader relicensing rights are ever required.
