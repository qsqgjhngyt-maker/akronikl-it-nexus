# Legal Strategy

## Status

**Foundation legal baseline v0.1.** This is a project legal strategy, not a substitute for jurisdiction-specific legal advice.

## Protection layers

Akronikl IT Nexus contains software, learning texts, datasets/structured catalogs, artwork/UI assets, brand identity, secrets, and third-party materials. A single license cannot govern all of them safely.

Copyright protects original expression such as code, text and artwork, but generally not the underlying idea of an “AI mentor + Skill Graph + multilingual learning platform”. The strategy can restrict copying and commercial reuse of protected material, but cannot guarantee exclusivity over independently implemented ideas.

Protection therefore combines copyright, carefully scoped licenses, brand/trademark protection, provenance evidence, technical secret management, and a third-party rights registry.

## Current safe state

No public root `LICENSE` is activated until ownership, third-party materials and contributor rights are audited. Default copyright remains in place in the meantime.

## Preconditions before license activation

- identify rights holders and copyright notices;
- audit the C++ baseline and source PDFs/materials;
- inventory third-party dependencies and notices;
- define contributor/relicensing rights;
- review the selected software license with counsel for the relevant jurisdictions;
- perform trademark clearance before filing.

## Research basis

WIPO copyright guidance; GitHub repository licensing guidance; PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0; CC BY-NC-SA 4.0; OSI Open Source Definition; and relevant Russian Civil Code provisions on works, software, open licenses and trademarks.
